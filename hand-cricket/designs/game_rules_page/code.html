Basics: How to Play

Input Your Move

Select a number from 1 to 6. You and the opponent play simultaneously.

2

Batting & Scoring

If numbers match, you are OUT. Otherwise, your number is added to your runs.

3

Chasing the Target

Roles swap after a wicket. Match the opponent's number to get them out before they beat your score.
